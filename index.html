        <h2 class="title is-3">Abstract</h2>

        <div class="content has-text-justified abstract-box">

          <p>

            While Large Language Models (LLMs) excel in language-based agentic tasks, their applicability to unseen, non-linguistic environments (e.g., symbolic or spatial tasks) remains limited. We demonstrate the primary bottleneck is the prohibitive cost of exploration: mastering these tasks requires extensive trial-and-error, which is computationally unsustainable for parameter-heavy LLMs.

          </p>

          <p>

            To address this, we propose <strong>SCOUT (Sub-Scale Collaboration On Unseen Task)</strong>, a novel framework that decouples exploration from exploitation. We employ lightweight "scouts" (e.g., small MLPs) to probe environmental dynamics at a speed and scale far exceeding LLMs. The collected trajectories are utilized to bootstrap the LLM via Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT), followed by multi-turn Reinforcement Learning (RL).

          </p>

          <p>

            Empirically, SCOUT enables a Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct model to achieve an average score of <strong>0.86</strong>, significantly outperforming proprietary models, including Gemini-2.5-Pro (0.60), while saving about <strong>60%</strong> GPU hours.

          </p>

        </div>

      </div>

    </div>

  </div>

</section>


<section class="section">

  <div class="container is-max-desktop">

    <h2 class="title is-3 section-title has-text-centered">Methodology</h2>

    <div class="content has-text-justified">

      <p>

        The SCOUT pipeline consists of three stages designed to transfer environmental physics from lightweight networks to heavy reasoning models:

      </p>

      <ul>

        <li><strong>Exploration Stage:</strong> Lightweight scouts (MLP/CNN) interact with the environment using efficient RL (DQN/PPO) to collect expert trajectories.</li>

        <li><strong>Distillation Stage:</strong> A deterministic <em>Textualizer</em> converts symbolic trajectories into language dialogue, which is used to SFT the LLM.</li>

        <li><strong>Evolving Stage:</strong> The SFT-warmup LLM undergoes multi-turn PPO training to activate latent world knowledge and refine reasoning strategies.</li>

      </ul>
